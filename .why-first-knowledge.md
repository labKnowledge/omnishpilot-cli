# Why-first knowledge — OmnishPilot

Updated: 2026-10-29

## Golden Circle

**WHY**  
Heavy work should finish while you sleep — and you should wake to proof, not hope.

**HOW**  
Own the *outcome* of unattended agent work on your machine: keep going when you’re gone, refuse false greens, tell you the truth in the morning. (Sandbox, failover, acceptance, wake-lock, morning pack are expressions of this — not the message.)

**WHAT**  
OmnishPilot — free local autonomy harness (`npm i -g omnishpilot`). Linux / macOS / Windows.

## Competitive white space

Market (Claude Routines / Cursor Automations / cron wrappers) sells **triggers**: schedule, cloud, autopilot, “eliminate repetitive tasks.”

**Gap we own:** the morning after — babysitting vs finished work; green status vs proof.

Status quo pain (from `docs/research-scheduling-pain-points.md`): silent failure, approval stalls, hangs, rate limits, laptop sleep, false success.

## Language that works

- babysitting / wake up to done / proof not hope / green ≠ done / close the laptop / finished work / free · local · yours

## Language to avoid in marketing

- Feature laundry: sandbox, bwrap, YOLO, verifier, webhook, failover, CLI flags
- “AI-powered” / “next-gen” / “seamless”
